Editor’s Note: The Greenish Eye

2025-08-05 Thinking Talking

Sometimes it is hard to escape the arrogance and prejudice stamped into an era. I can only express myself through music.

Unicode characters appeared long ago, but only with the age of large models has humanity’s digitised knowledge begun to approach unity.

Characters, words, grammar, and semantics—countless symbols flow into history through self-narration, and countless people are forced to choose what they want to become.

What do I want to express? What can I express?

I cannot figure it out.

I believe music can always be deconstructed.

So I wrote One in Fifty Thousand , Long Goodbye , The Place Never Reached , and The Black Cat Appears in Daylight . The last one deconstructs everything in my music.

But can this really be called creation? Do I truly have thoughts of my own? Can I think better than a large model?

Thinking is thankless, much like my nearly thirty years of life: ordinary, average, and full of idle talk like my bio .

The idle talk of an ordinary person.

“Did you know that one in every twenty-five people has an antisocial personality? Antisocial is neither praise nor insult.” I heard it say.

Yes, exactly. But tell me: how would you define society?
