0xA1_3 - Decoding

2026-09-29

Inner voices

Colored sentences are voices in the story. Click one to see who is speaking.

    Mission
    Goal
    Read the contents of the black box.
    You are
    You are still Fang Li. The black box has been printed and is on the pallet.
    Clue
    The MV of a song. You need to pause, drag the progress bar, or even watch it frame by frame - there are frame-by-frame buttons below the video.

    When you return to the lab after dinner, the 3D printer has stopped.

    What was placed on the tray was not one black box, but two. One orange and one gray.

    “…buy one get one free?”

    “There are two black boxes.” Kate placed them side by side. “The orange one is the flight data recorder, which records data. The gray one is the cabin audio recorder, which records sounds.”

    [In this regard, the two worlds are the same. 】 Cloak said.

    Kate connected the orange box to the tablet. A music video for a song appeared on the tablet.

    “Is it something from another world?”

    “Data loggers only record one kind of data in this world.” Kate knocked on the orange box, “Time. All the clocks in the video will stop at the same moment. It’s so short that you’ll miss it if you blink.”

    You looked at the clock spinning on the screen and your eyes started to ache.

    〖Just watch it frame by frame, no one will laugh at you. 〗Emotional theory.

    FDR / TIME

    Format: hour:minute, for example 1:23.

    Tip 1 · Direction

    When the clock stops, the hands of all the clocks point to the same time, and the hands are spinning around in the next few frames.

    Tip 2 · Method

    There are three places with the most clocks: the clock by the bed when singing “Nonsense and Random Thoughts”, the clock hanging on the tree when singing “Hurry”, and the clock tower city when it rains. After pausing, use the “− 1 frame / + 1 frame” button below the video to search slowly, or turn on 0.25× slow playback.

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    Around 0:39. The stubby black hour hand is between 2 and 3, and the slender red minute hand points to 43 minutes.

    The orange box is unresponsive.

    The orange box clicked.

    “Two forty-three…” You muttered, “The plane took off at 00:41, that’s wrong.”

    “Who said this was the time on the clock?” Kate dragged the progress bar to 2:43.

    Snowy mountains. A lyric hit the screen: The fear in my heart.

    Then you saw a word that didn’t look right. Its font is different from other words, its color is also different, and it’s a little crooked. For a split second at 2:43 — just a split second, really — it became another word.

    FDR / GLYPH
    Tip 1 · Direction

    What’s wrong is the word “fear” in “fear in my heart”.

    Tip 2 · Method

    Stop the progress bar at 2:42, then use “+ 1 frame” to go back one frame at a time. The change only lasts two frames, at exactly 2:43.

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    The word it became is related to the weather.

    “That’s not it,” Kate said. “What did you see before it changed back?”

    “Rain.” You said, “Rain in my heart.”

    Kate didn’t answer. She connected the gray box to another instrument.

    “There’s no rain in this song,” you say.

    “What about after you finish singing?”

    You drag the progress bar to the end. The song has been sung, leaving only the production list and some very soft sounds.

    It’s the sound of rain.

    “The voice recorder records everything it hears,” Kate said, “including things that someone didn’t want it to hear.”

    [You can’t hear it with your ears. There is something missing in the sound of rain. What you want to look at is the frequency spectrum of this sound. 】

    〖Spectrum is to draw sound into a picture! The horizontal line is time, and the vertical line is pitch! 〗

    CVR / TRANSCRIPT

    Punctuation can be omitted.

    Tip 1 · Direction

    First click “Load Blog Archive Recording”. This is the sound in the gray box. The sound of rain is at the end of the entire recording.

    Tip 2 · Method

    The total length of the recording is approximately 263 seconds. Set the starting point at around 252 seconds, a duration of 10 seconds, and click “Decode”. The words are empty: look at where the color darkens, not where it lights up.

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    Two lines, four words each. The first line is related to the weather, and the second line is related to three meals a day.

    The gray box rustled like rain.

    “Look again,” Kate said.

    You pronounced that sentence.

    The gray box was quiet for a few seconds, then played a recording.

    “When the rain stops, let’s go home and eat.”

    A very low voice. It’s very low, but very close, as if the speaker is squatting next to the box.

    〖Who is this…〗

    [Not the people on the plane. 】

    “Isn’t this a recording from a plane?” You asked.

    Kate didn’t answer. She played the recording again.

    Then the third time.

    “…She remembered it wrong again.” Kate unplugged the gray box.

    “Who?”

    “A friend.”

    “Then why did she—”

    “She always thinks I’m her daughter.” Kate put the two boxes into her bag and zipped them up, “I’m not.”

    You didn’t say anything.

    [Sometimes, people put very important people in the wrong position. Because only that position is vacant. 】

    “Let’s go.” Kate picked up her bag, “The rain stops slowly in this world. We have other boxes to find.”
