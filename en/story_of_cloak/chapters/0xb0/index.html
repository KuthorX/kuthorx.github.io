    Final
    Goal
    Accompany Kate to complete her last case: find the Green Eye.
    You are
    yourself.
    Clue
    All the roads you’ve walked. A drawing by a seven-year-old child. An instrument that turns colors.

    Tavern in Dead Elephant.

    It’s still raining. Dark red, the kind that never stops.

    Everyone is here.

    Earfish is cleaning glasses behind the bar. Ah Mao was sitting on a high chair, with only two knives on his waist. The cloak was huddled in the gauze of the sofa. The sofa - known as Grandmaster in my head and Shenxiu in the hotel - was humming, occasionally pretending to be just a sofa. Shang Zheng and Lu Yu were sitting by the window. A gentleman in a tuxedo adjusts his Bluetooth speaker. Fang Li was nibbling on a piece of bread and looked like he hadn’t fully woken up yet. “Socrates” is holding three empty small black boxes.

    Kate stood in the middle of the pub.

    “My father said,” she said, “after eliminating other possibilities, the one that remains must be true.”

    She looked at the ear fish: “Your eyes are pale green.”

    “It’s just a color.” The ear fish said, “I’m not what you’re looking for.”

    She looked at the cloak. Cloak’s eyes are also pale green.

    【I’m just recording. ] Cloak said, [I don’t know how to read the recorded things. 】

    She looked at Ah Mao.

    “I can’t see green.” Ah Mao said, “You know, daughter.”

    Kate didn’t correct her.

    “Neither,” Kate said.

    She took a folded piece of paper from her pocket and spread it out on the bar.

    纸上是这么几行字：

    A crayon drawing. The sun, clouds, grass, a stick figure. A large green sky.

    *Kate, 7, to dad. *

    “When I was a kid, everyone said I painted the sky the wrong color,” Kate said. “Only my dad said it was correct. He said someone was watching.”

    She pushes the painting in front of you.

    Yes, you.

    HUE / SKY
    Tip 1 · Direction

    Just like in the “Solutions” chapter, turn the Hue slider.

    Tip 2 · Method

    The color of the hidden words this time is the original color of the sky: pale green. It’s a little more blue than pure green.

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    The slider is around 160°. You saw this word in the prologue: “Welcome to the world of _______.”

    Kate looks at you, waiting for you to look again.

    Grenyes。

    “This was the last word left by my dad,” Kate said. “I’ve been looking for it for years and never knew what it was.”

    【It is a place. 】 Cloak said.

    〖This is where we live! 〗

    / is also where you are reading. /

    The whole tavern fell silent. Even the sound of rain became quieter.

    Kate turns around and looks at you, “What are Grenyes?”

    GRENYES What are
    Tip 1 · Direction

    All instruments display articles in the same place.

    Tip 2 · Method

    “Full Moon”, “Water Ball”, “Two Parts in One Hundred Thousand”… where are they placed?

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    The website you are reading now. Four words, starting with “this”.

    “Not there,” Kate said. “Closer.”

    “This blog.” Kate repeated.

    She raised her head and looked straight out of the screen for the first time.

    “So, last question.” She said, “What color are your eyes?”

    THE LAST CASE
    Tip 1 · Direction

    In this story, whoever’s eyes are seen as a certain color are being looked at by you.

    Tip 2 · Method

    Fang Li, Nishimiya, the fake Supreme Copper, Kate in the eyes of “Socrates” - you have seen the same color in their eyes.

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    The name of this game, the first two words.

    Kate looks at you. She is waiting.

    Pale green.

    Kate smiled.

    In everyone’s eyes, her eyes were also pale green at that moment.

    Because you’re looking at her.

    “Found it,” she said.

    Outside the window, the rain stopped.

    No one spoke. The ear fish put down the cup. The cloak peeked out from the gauze. The square piece of bread fell to the ground.

    Ah Mao stood up first and stretched.

    “Go home for dinner,” she said.

    Kate didn’t correct her.

    She folded the painting, put it back in her pocket, and followed Ah Mao out of the tavern.

    When she reached the door, she turned back and waved to you.

    Thanks for playing, see you next time.
