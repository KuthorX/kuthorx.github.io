0xB0 - The Last Distribution

2025-08-30
脑内声音

带颜色的句子是方立脑子里的声音。点一下，看是谁在说话。

    Mission · The Last Distribution
    Goal
    Distribute the final message and decide who receives it.
    You are
    A temporary courier between worlds.
    Clue
    The delivery order, the timestamps, and the empty address.

    The last package has no sender and no destination. Its label contains only a sequence of times and the instruction: deliver when the rain stops.

    Fang Li, Kate, Cloak, and the voices each interpret the label differently. The package passes through the tavern, the lunar institute, and the Dead Elephant. At every stop, someone insists that the message belongs to someone else.

    Archive · The message

    When the rain stops, go home for dinner.

    The sentence is ordinary. Its distribution is not. It is addressed to the person who has been placed in the wrong position for the longest time.

    The courier is also the recipient. The final distribution completes the loop.

    The package opens. Inside is no object, only a blank page. The blank page reflects the reader’s face, and the story ends where the next reader begins.
