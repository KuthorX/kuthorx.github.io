0xA1_5 - World Line

2026-09-30

Inner voices

Colored sentences are voices in the story. Click one to see who is speaking.

    Quest · World Line
    Goal
    Calibrate the world line deviation meter and let Fang Li return to the original world.
    You are
    Fang Li, researcher—at least for now.
    Clue
    Two earlier posts in this blog: "I'm back, but "I" will start again soon" and "Blog version II is coming" .

    All three boxes were found.

    That night, as soon as you lay down in the dormitory, the voice in your head came back.

    [The coordinates have been fixed. ] Cloak said, [You can go back. 】

    〖Congratulations! Although I’m a little reluctant to part with it…〗

    “Go back? Back to 2025?”

    【Go back to your original world. But before projection, an instrument must be calibrated. It’s in the lab. 】

    The next day, you went to the laboratory alone.

    The instrument was old, like an old radio, with a round dial on the front and hands wandering around in the red area. There is a line of words engraved under the dial, but a few words are missing:

    The deviation of the world line is still within the range of ____

    〖As long as the pointer returns to the green area, you are good to go. 〗

    [This sentence was written by a person in another world. When he wrote it, he was also preparing to start again. 】

    WORLDLINE / GAUGE
    Tip 1 · Direction

    The first article in the clue: ““I” is back, but “I” will start again soon.”

    Tip 2 · Method

    Look for the words “world line” in the article. That sentence is in italics.

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    It means “can still be seen and measured”.

    The pointer trembled even more in the red area.

    The pointer slowly slid back to the green area.

    “Senior?” Kate opened the door and came in, “What are you doing here so early in the morning?”

    You didn’t hide it from her.

    Kate was silent for a while after listening.

    “So, you are not Fang Li in the first place.”

    “…Hmm.”

    “I know,” she said, “I’ve known it since the first day you asked, ‘What is the little black box?’”

    She walked to the instrument and looked at the dial: “What else is missing?”

    [There is still one sentence left to confirm the password. ] Cloak said, [The man in the other world left his last words when he was setting up a new place. 】

    WORLDLINE / PASSPHRASE
    Tip 1 · Direction

    Second post in the thread: “Blog Version II is Coming.”

    Tip 2 · Method

    That article was short. The password is its last sentence.

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    A “new” something. You are in it now.

    The instrument buzzed but did not start.

    The instrument started.

    [Before the projection begins, let me tell you one thing. ] Cloak said, [In another dimension, you also have a name called 西宫. You’ll be on an open mic stage and tell some not-so-funny jokes. 】

    〖It’s actually quite funny! 〗

    “Senior.” Kate stood at the door, “See you next time.”

    “See you next time.”

    Kate looks into your eyes.

    The pale green color that always made her wonder if she was wearing contact lenses gradually faded away.

    Then, standing in front of the instrument was just Fang Li.

    A graduate student who is about to graduate. She blinked and looked at Kate: “…why am I in the laboratory?”

    “Senior is sleepy.” Kate said, “Let’s go and eat first.”
