    Task · Tavern
    Target
    Spend your first night in a tavern with a cloak.
    You are
    bystander.
    Clue
    Fireclaw in the character card; and the end of the novel One in Fifty Thousand . Theme song: Listen to on NetEase Cloud Music.

    After learning to move, the cloak passed through the jungle, and the first place it encountered was a quiet tavern.

    The pub owner is a bear. He had one injured ear and the other ear was shaped like a fish gill. His eyes were pale green.

    “Is this your past?" The bear looked at a picture on the wall. Jungle, rain, mud puddle.

    “Only part of it.” The cloak had learned to speak. “A friend painted it for me.”

    The bear spread out its paws and showed the cloak a few sparkling flat objects: “It looks like your eyes, greenish-green. We call it money. Green, also called green coins.”

    “ * * green * * .”

    “… you don’t have to repeat what I said.”

    “Alright.”

    The doorbell rang. A kitten came in with three knives hanging from her waist.

    “Long time no see, Fireclaw .” said the bear.

    “Just call me Cat. I don’t like the name Fireclaw.” The kitten jumped onto the high chair, “I still have to call you a fish?”

    “That’s the way to call it now.” The bear - Earfish - poured her a glass of wine.

    Cat glanced at the cloak: “New friend? What’s your name?”

    “ * * What is it called * * .”

    “? What’s wrong with this guy?”

    “It only repeats the last word of a sentence." The earfish said, “It’s still a blank piece of paper.”

    “White paper?” Ah Cat squinted. “I’ve lived for 80 years. I’ve never seen anything like this.”

    She let out a low roar. The voice came out of such a small body, but it was as low as if the entire tavern was resonating. The body of the cloak trembled.

    “Control your abilities, Fireclaw.”

    “Hmm.” Cat put down the glass. “That’s simple. Just let it * move * and you’ll know.”

    She pulled a silver knife from her waist and stabbed it into the tavern floor.

    The cloak widened its eyes.

    The knife did not move.

    But the cloak felt like it was moving.

    No - the cloak thought - it wasn’t me who moved.

    The movement should be ____.

    SHAKE THE EARTH
    Tip 1 · Direction

    This silver knife was called [Earth Shaking]. Its function is written in the character card of Fireclaw.

    Tip 2 · Method

    [Earth Shaking] can make “inanimate objects” move. Here, what is inanimate and big enough to make the cloak feel “moving”?

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    The place they’re in right now.

    The cloak wobbled and fainted even more.

    The whole tavern slowly rose in the rain.

    “… I said you,” the earfish wiped the cup, “treat your son like this, is it really okay?”

    “Hmm.” Cat left the knife on the floor. “Everyone is dead. This knife, like the other two, is just a knife.”

    The earfish did not answer. He looked at the knife, and after fifty thousandths of a second he said:

    “It’s easy to love all of humanity. Loving someone __ is hard."

    EARFISH / QUOTE
    Tip 1 · Direction

    The phrase appeared at the end of One Fifty Thousand, but a few words were replaced with strange symbols.

    Tip 2 · Method

    Find the sentence beginning with “Love all”: “Love all humanity is easy; love one person is hard.” The missing word before the symbols is “humanity,” and the missing word after them is…

    Hint 3 · Almost the answer

    Same as the first character of “humanity.”

    Earfish shakes his head and continues wiping the cup.

    “ person .” the Cloak repeats.

    It was the first time it had repeated a character that was not at the end of a sentence.

    A-Mao stares at it for a long time. “…It is not just blank paper.”

    “Hmm.” Earfish says. “It is learning.”

    Cloak did not hear the rest. The tavern rose too high, and it fainted.

    Earfish carries it to a sofa in the corner. The sofa wobbles and wraps the cloak in a fine star net.

    / Star net. Just learned! /

    “You learned something again,” Earfish says.

    Outside the tavern, rain kept falling. Someone hummed a simple song.

    File · Green fire and audible color

    Several elements burn green: copper is blue-green, boron bright green, and barium yellow-green (see Supreme Copper character card).

    Flame color comes from the spectrum; the shape of sound comes from the spectrogram. Both are frequencies, both are waves. Cloak learned to hear two thousand sounds in the jungle, and learned to “see” them.

    Fireclaw’s second form is called “Canaan.” The low growl that made the whole tavern resonate is her ability: sound waves.
