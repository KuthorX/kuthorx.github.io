    After learning to move, Cloak walked through the jungle, and the first place he encountered was a quiet tavern.

    The tavern keeper is a bear. He has one injured ear and the other is shaped like a fish gill. His eyes are pale green.

    “Is this your past?” Xiong looked at a painting on the wall. The paintings are of jungles, rain, and mud puddles.

    “It’s just a part.” Cloak has learned to speak, “The painting was painted for me by a friend.”

    The bear opened its paws and showed the cloak a few shiny flat objects: “It’s very similar to your eyes, pale green. We call them money. The green ones are also called green coins.”

    “Green Coin.”

    “…You don’t have to repeat what I said.”

    “…Okay.”

    The doorbell rang. What came in was a kitten with three knives hanging from its waist.

    “Long time no see, Fire Claw .” Xiong said.

    “Just call me Cat. I don’t like the name Firepaw very much.” The kitten jumped on the high chair, “I still have to call you Fish?”

    “That’s what it needs to be called now.” The bear — earfish — poured her a glass of wine.

    Ah Mao glanced at the cloak: “New friend? What is your name?”

    “What is it called.”

    “? What’s going on with this guy?”

    “It only repeats the last word of a sentence.” Earfish said, “It’s still a blank piece of paper.”

    “Blank paper?” Ah Mao narrowed his eyes, “I have lived for eighty years and have never seen anything like this.”

    She let out a low growl. The voice came from such a small body, but it was so deep that it seemed like the entire tavern was resonating. Cloak’s body trembled.

    “Control your powers, Firepaw.”

    “Hum.” Ah Mao put down the wine glass, “That’s easy, just let it move and you’ll know.”

    She pulled a silver knife from her waist and stabbed it into the tavern floor.

    Cloak’s eyes widened.

    The knife didn’t move.

    But Cloak felt himself moving.

    No - thought Cloak - it wasn’t me who moved.

    Tip 2 · Method

    The cloak swayed, making him even more dizzy.

    “Hate.” Ah Mao left the knife on the floor. “Everyone is dead. This knife is just like the other two. It’s just a knife.”

    Earfish didn’t answer. He looked at the knife, and after one fifty-thousandth of a second, he said:

    “It is easy to love all mankind. It is difficult to love one __.”

    EARFISH / QUOTE
    Tip 1 · Direction

    This sentence appears at the end of “One in Fifty Thousand”, but several words have been replaced with strange symbols.

    Tip 2 · Method

    Find the sentence that begins with “爱全”: “It’s easy to love the whole \x1d\x1e, but it’s hard to love \x1f all!”. What’s missing in the front is “human beings”, what’s missing in the back is…

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    Same as the first word of “human being”.

    Archives · Green Fire, and Audible Colors

    Several elements burn green: copper is blue-green, boron is bright green, and barium is yellow-green (see the character card of Supreme Copper ).

    The color of the flame comes from the spectrum, and the appearance of the sound comes from the acoustic spectrum. Both are frequencies, both are waves. Cloak learned to listen to two thousand sounds in the jungle, and also learned to “see” them.

    Fire Claw’s second form is called “Canaan”. That low roar that resonated throughout the tavern was her ability: Sound Wave.
