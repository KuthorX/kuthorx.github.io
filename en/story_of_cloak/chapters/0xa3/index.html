0xA3 - Explosion

2025-08-30

Inner voices

Colored sentences are voices in the story. Click one to see who is speaking.

    Mission · Explosion
    Goal
    Accompany Nishimiya through this open mic performance, don’t let the audience find you backstage.
    You are a staff member of
    backend. You're watching from behind the scenes.
    Clue
    Every word said on stage; and the song he played.

    (An open mic scene.)

    (A gentleman in a tuxedo jumped up to the stage. He was holding a can of iced Coke in his hand, shaking it as he walked.)

    (Behind the scenes, you look at him.)

    Nishimiya (bows): Hello everyone, my name is Nishimiya and I am a musician.

    (There is silence.)

    Nishimiya (smile): Just kidding. In fact, I am a senior researcher at a research institute.

    (Whispering to each other. Someone in the front row said “Wow”.)

    Nishimiya: Speaking of which, guess how old I am? Friendly reminder: I look a bit young.

    Audience A (holding throat): 100 years old.

    Audience B (roaring): You must be 35 years old to be so handsome!

    Audience C (blushing): 18 years old.

    Audience D (slowly): Haha, maybe 40.

    Nishimiya (raises one finger): Okay. I’ll give you three clues -

    Nishimiya: First, among the four of you, only one person guessed the ten digits correctly, and only one person guessed the ones digits correctly, but no one guessed completely correctly.

    Nishimiya: Second, I am not yet forty**.

    Nishimiya (looking at Audience C): Third, this audience member - you guessed it.

    OPEN MIC / AGE
    Tip 1 · Direction

    Write the tens and ones digits of the four guesses separately: 100 (tens 0, units 0), 35, 18, 40.

    Tip 2 · Method

    Two people guessed the ones digit of 0, so if the answer’s ones digit was 0, there would be two people who guessed the ones digit correctly - no way. The ones digit can only be 5 or 8.

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    Except for 5 and 8, which are too small, the candidates are only 15, 38, 45, and 48. Screen again with “less than forty” and “bigger than 18.”

    Nishimiya (shaking his head): No, no.

    Nishinomiya: Thirty-eight.

    (Audience B whistles.)

    Nishimiya: Do you know? The longest living cat in history was also thirty-eight years old. My English code name is One White Tiger - a very big cat.

    Nishimiya: Okay, let’s not talk about age for now. Today I mainly want to talk to you about music. Listen to this passage and guess what it says.

    (Nishimiya took out a small Bluetooth speaker and tinkered with it for a while. There was a low tuning sound.)

    Audience B (roaring): I know this! White noise! No, FM sound!

    Nishimiya: 〖Stop arguing, you CiiaKua. 〗

    Audience B (flustered): What was that just now?

    Nishimiya (smile): It’s nothing, don’t think too much about it.

    (Piano sound.)

    Audience D (raising eyebrows): Minor key.

    (Nishimiya nodded and placed the shaken can of iced Coke next to the stereo.)

    Nishimiya:〖Listen slowly, everyone. This is “Black Cat Appears in Daylight”. There’s going to be a big explosion here. And among the four of you, no one can survive. 〗

    Lyric Book · "Black Cat Appears in the Daytime" (Lyrics: Five Thousand Moonlight Nights)

    If you still miss me/then come and recreate yesterday

    Like the black cat appearing in the daytime / extending its claws and breaking the experiment

    The alleys of the city, whose flower petals are hidden / The decoration in the paradise, the empty lies

    Stories from the horizon are too far away from me / Why don’t you open a can of iced Coke and drink it? It’s awesome

    If you no longer miss me/then come and preview tomorrow

    Like a black cat appearing in the daytime / opening its mouth and yawning

    Late evening cherry blossoms, melting snow and forgotten / Broken paradise, licked by the evening breeze

    The distant horizon has nothing to do with me / But the cola bubbles and sprays into a pool

    OPEN MIC / BOOM
    Tip 1 · Direction

    As soon as Nishimiya came on stage, he held something in his hand and kept shaking it.

    Tip 2 · Method

    Open the lyrics book and look for sentences related to the thing in his hand. There are two sentences, one is about opening, the other is…

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    The last sentence of the second paragraph begins with “Coke”.

    (Nothing happens. The music continues.)

    (When singing “Coke bubbled and sprayed all over the pool,” Nishimiya pulled the tab.)

    (Tsk——)

    (The iced Coke that was shaken all the way sprayed all over the four people in the front row.)

    (Silence. Then roars of laughter. Four people wipe their faces and curse. No one “survived” - at least not decently.)

    Nishimiya (puts away the speaker, turns around, pointing at you): [And you, you will see…]

    Nishimiya: [Kate Wall, you will see. 】

    (You are stunned. You are just a background worker.)

    (But at that moment, his eyes were pale green.)
