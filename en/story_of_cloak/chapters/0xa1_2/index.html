0xA1_2 - Full Moon

2026-09-27
脑内声音

带颜色的句子是方立脑子里的声音。点一下，看是谁在说话。

    Mission
    Objective
    Find the penultimate sentence of the “Full Moon” blog, enter it into the instrument, and print the black box.
    You are
    Fang Li, a researcher. Kate is your intern.
    Clue
    “Full Moon” is a real article on this blog. Open it, read it, and try to click it.

    Kate leads you into a laboratory.

    【First, one thing.】The Cloak’s voice sounds. 【The 2014 you are in now is different from the one you remember—even some scientific principles are different.】

    【Take finding the black box. In your world, you search the scene; in this world, the “scene” is a web page.】

    〖Sounds ridiculous, right?!〗Emotion blurts out.

    “Senior, you still remember how to use this equipment, right?” Kate asks.

    There are three things on the lab bench: a tablet, a keyboard and monitor, and a 3D printer.

    The tablet displays a blog post .

    “…Isn’t this just a tablet?”

    “You really do not remember.” Kate sighs. “Listen: the black box is hidden in this blog. We need to find its penultimate sentence, enter it on the keyboard, and the printer will print the black box.”

    “Why the penultimate sentence?”

    “Because the last sentence is written for the reader.” Kate says it matter-of-factly. “The penultimate sentence is for that person.”

    You decide not to ask further.

    【A hint: this blog starts out incomplete. The ending is hidden; fix the article first.】

    BLACK BOX / INPUT

    Enter the complete sentence; punctuation may be omitted.

    Hint 1 · Direction

    The ending of “Full Moon” is initially invisible. Some things are “waiting for you to act.”

    Hint 2 · Method

    Several sentences in the article tremble slightly; click them to fix them. You can also click the cheeseburger roll image until it is eaten. Once everything is fixed and eaten, the ending appears.

    Hint 3 · Almost the answer

    The final sentence is “Thanks for playing; see you next time.” The sentence before it begins with “Luckily, among the many…”.

    The tablet emits an unfriendly beep.

    “It’s fine. This system has no login protection; try anything,” Kate says.

    The tablet emits a cheerful beep.

    The 3D printer rumbles to life.

    “That’s our senior!” Kate laughs. “Printing will take about an hour. Come on, let’s eat.”

    〖Food!〗

    【…You two get along quite well.】
