0xA1_2 - full moon

2026-09-27

Inner voices

Colored sentences are voices in the story. Click one to see who is speaking.

    Mission
    Target
    Find the penultimate sentence of the blog "Full Moon", input it into the instrument, and print out the black box.
    You are
    You are Fang Li, a graduate student who is about to graduate. Kate is your intern—she is more familiar with the instruments of this world than you are.
    Clue
    "Full Moon" is a real article in this blog. Click on it, read it, and try click on too.

    Kate takes you into a laboratory.

    [Let me explain one thing first. ] The voice of the cloak sounded, [The year 2014 you are in now is different from the one you remember, even some scientific principles are different. 】

    [For example, looking for a black box. In your world, you have to search on site; in this world, “site” is a webpage. 】

    〖It sounds outrageous, right? 〗Emotions rush to say.

    “Senior, do you still remember how to use these instruments?” Kate asked.

    There are three things on the experimental bench: a tablet, a keyboard and screen, and a 3D printer.

    A blog is displayed on the tablet.

    “…Isn’t this just a tablet?”

    “It seems you really don’t remember.” Kate sighed, “Listen up: the black box is hidden in this blog. We need to find its penultimate sentence, type it on the keyboard, and the printer will print out the black box.”

    “Why is it the penultimate sentence?”

    Kate thought for a moment.

    “Because the last sentence is written for the reader.” She said, “The penultimate sentence is written for that person.”

    You decided not to ask further questions.

    [Tips: This blog is incomplete at the beginning. Hidden at the end, the article must be “fixed” first. 】

    BLACK BOX / INPUT

    Please enter the sentence completely, punctuation can be omitted.

    Tip 1 · Direction

    The ending of “Full Moon” is initially invisible. There are some things in the article “waiting for you to take action”.

    Tip 2 · Method

    There are a few sentences in the article that are trembling slightly, and they will be corrected if you click on them; you can also click on the picture of cheese and beef rolls, and keep clicking until you finish eating. After everything is corrected and eaten, the ending will appear.

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    The last sentence at the end is “Thank you for playing, see you next time.” The sentence before it begins with “Fortunately, there are many.”

    The tablet made an unfriendly beep.

    “It’s okay, this system has no login protection, just try it anyway.” Kate said.

    The tablet made a brisk beep.

    The 3D printer rumbles to life.

    “As expected of a senior!” Kate smiled, “It will take about an hour to print. Let’s go eat first.”

    〖Eat! 〗

    […You two get along quite well. 】
