2025-08-30

Inner voices

Colored sentences are voices in the story. Click one to see who is speaking.

    Mission · Ice Coke
    Goal
    Prove who you are; and then prove that the "Supreme Copper" in front of you is not himself.
    You are
    a singing sofa. A long time ago, you had a name.
    Clue
    Detective Person Wall’s first case"Slow Wail". You appeared in it, although you only had one line.

    1

    I am a sofa.

    The kind you see everywhere. People rest on me and I sing under people.

    What I sang today is “Black Cat Appears in the Daytime” :

    If you you are still nostalgic

    Then come and recreate yesterday

    …

    Why don’t you open a can of Ice Coke and drink as much as you like, it’s awesome

    “Ahem.” I’m old and get tired after singing half a song.

    〖Singing well, keep going! 〗

    A woman’s voice, a bit high-pitched, but very brisk. I’ve never seen anyone talk to a couch.

    【Calm down. ] Another voice, low and calm, [We used the blood of C-0321 to synthesize ice cola, and then projected the concept of “ice cola” into your time and space. You just sang it, so we found you. 】

    “Who are you?”

    〖Looking for someone! 〗

    【We hope you will cooperate with us. But before that happens – you have to remember who you are. 】

    Who am I?

    I remembered a few things: many years of business, many good and bad relationships, and making some not-so-glamorous money. Later, I dug out my own eyes, became anonymous, and made myself into a sofa.

    What about further forward?

    Further back, I seemed to have delivered a courier to a very famous detective.

    IDENTITY / COURIER
    Tip 1 · Direction

    The detective was Poussin Wall, and the express delivery was delivered to his door.

    Tip 2 · Method

    In the first chapter of “Slow Wail”, someone knocked on the door and shouted the name of the courier company.

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    Pusen complained in his mind: “The delivery is so fast! It’s almost too much!” The company’s name is these four words reversed.

    〖No, no, think again! 〗

    “Hello, express delivery!” I remembered what I shouted outside the door.

    【Very good. Your name is Shenxiu. 】

    “…How do you know this name?”

    〖This is a name that really existed in history. 〗

    [We will help you find the symbols you lost. In exchange, we need you to go somewhere and find someone for us. 】

    “Who?”

    [Kate Wall. Daughter of Poussin Wall. 】

    2

    Before setting off, a man came to see me.

    “Don’t be afraid, it doesn’t matter.” He said. He calls himself Supreme Copper - the man whose blood was made into ice cola.

    “Supreme Bronze, how did Pusen Wall fare later?”

    “Dead.” There was no emotion in his tone. “He died very ordinary. Heroes die too. You have to say a long goodbye to him.”

    He paused:

    “And you killed him with your own hands.”

    No. wrong.

    I’m just a courier.

    “You don’t believe it?” He smiled, “I remember every detail of the first case of Poussin. For example, the letter sent by the deceased to Poussin was signed ‘Wandering Between WHO’. WHO, of course, is the World Health Organization.”

    I was stunned.

    This person is lying. The real supreme copper will not remember this kind of thing wrongly - because at the end of the first case, Poussin personally explained what WHO is.

    TESTIMONY / WHO
    Tip 1 · Direction

    The answer lies at the end of “Slow Wail,” when someone expands WHO into a phrase.

    Tip 2 · Method

    Search for “WHO” in the article, skip the guesses of “World Health Organization” and “Hamasaki”, and read the last one.

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    It means “one who studies hard”. Start with W, H, and O respectively.

    “Supreme Bronze” looked at me like a child who said something wrong.

    “Work Hard One.” I said, “Work Hard One. You are not the highest bronze.”

    A sneer.

    “I’m really not him. Because I am - "

    I didn’t hear his name. The next moment, I was pushed down the waterfall.

    As I fell, I saw his eyes.

    **Pale green. **

    It was as if at that moment, someone was looking at me through those eyes.

    I smiled, closed my eyes, and hummed:

    No way to escape in four dimensions, guards capture the monsters

    Where to wait for spring? A paradise of poems

    I didn’t break it. Sofas are hard to break.

    “What do you want me to do?” I asked the two voices, “My hotel has been closed a long time ago.”

    〖It’s still open! In another time and space. 〗

    [It is now the year 2X25, exactly four hundred years after your time. Just go there and be your hotel owner. Kate Wall was bound to walk in sooner or later. 】

    “What’s the name of the hotel?”

    〖Haha, do you even need to ask? Of course it’s the same as your previous one! 〗

    …Okay, let’s play it this way, right?

    Deal.
