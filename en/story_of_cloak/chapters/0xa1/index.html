2025-08-30
Inner voices

Colored sentences are voices in the story. Click one to see who is speaking.

    Mission
    Goals
    Find out where you are and who you are.
    You are
    and you don’t know yet.
    Clue
    There are no puzzles in this chapter, just read on.

    Your phone lights up, and there is a line of text on the screen:

    It’s not difficult to play a character. You just choose and do it. If you don’t act, you are nothing. come on.

    You can’t understand. You don’t even remember when you fell asleep.

    “Fang Li, why are you so dazed?”

    Someone is calling you. You raised your head: bunk beds, four desks, and “Advanced Mathematics” spread out on the desks. A college dormitory.

    And you are very sure that your name is not Fang Li.

    “Here, I bought it for you.” The person calling you is your roommate. She thrust something into your hand, a panda stuffed animal.

    “…Jingjing?”

    The mascot of the Beijing Olympics. Your heart sinks: Is this 2008?

    File · Who is Jingjing

    One of the five “Fuwa” mascots for the 2008 Beijing Olympics. Your roommate is just teasing you - you’ll find out later what year it is.

    [Don’t be afraid, it doesn’t matter. 】

    a voice. Not your roommates, not yourself. It’s as calm as an instruction manual, ringing directly in your head.

    “Who?” you blurted out.

    “? Me.” The roommate looked confused, “Are you confused by your sleep?”

    〖She can’t hear us. 〗

    Another voice. Unlike the one just now, this one sounds more panicked than you.

    You decide to ignore them for now. The focus is not on “who I am” but on “who I should be now”.

    You glance at the bookshelf: there are no postgraduate entrance examination questions. very good. Then there is a draft of the graduation thesis on the table, which is written “Master’s Thesis”.

    …not so good. You are a graduate student, and you are a graduate student who is about to graduate.

    【One more thing. ] A voice like an instruction manual added, [This body is a female body. 】

    〖Cloak! Don’t tell her so much at once! 〗

    [She will find out sooner or later. 】

    Okay, you remember: the calm one is called cloak, and the panicked one… let’s call it emotion for now.

    Archives · What exactly are these two voices

    In the world where this story takes place, there is a chip called K-1647. The robot equipped with it will have two “modules” talking to each other in its brain: the review module, which is recorded with [ ]; the emotion module, which is recorded with 〖〗. What the review module says can usually be taken as fact.

    Earlier stories were written in “One in 100,000” and “One in 50,000” . Not reading it will not affect the game at all.

    “Fang Li!” Your roommate patted you, “If you don’t leave, it will be too late. If you don’t do today’s experiment, the little black box will be snatched away by others!”

    “Little black box?”

    “Okay, it’s not called a little black box.” She shrugged and suddenly became serious, “Listen, this is not 2008. I bought that panda in the second-hand market. Now -”

    You take out your phone.

    **March 9, 2014. **

    A notification hung at the top of the screen: *Malaysian Airlines flight MH370 disappeared with 239 people on board. *

    Archives · MH370

    At 0:41 a.m. on March 8, 2014, Malaysia Airlines Flight 370 took off from Kuala Lumpur and was scheduled to fly to Beijing. It lost contact with the ground less than an hour after takeoff. Years of searches since then have failed to find the main wreckage, and it remains one of the biggest mysteries in aviation history.

    What I’m talking about here is the plane in the real world. And where you are now is another 2014.

    【Your hands are not yours. 】 Cloak said.

    You bow your head. There is a very shallow scar on the back of your hand, you can’t remember when it was done.

    〖…That’s because the coordinates were calculated wrong. 〗Emotion said, 〖I’m sorry. 〗

    [Don’t tell her yet. ] Cloak said, [You can only stay here until it is repaired. 】

    [Use the identity “Fang Li”. 】

    The sound disappeared.

    You took a deep breath. good. Then let’s face Fang Li first.

    At this moment, the dormitory door was pushed open.

    “Catherine Wall, the daughter of Pusen Wall, officially reports for duty today!” The person at the door put her hands on her hips, “Just call me Kate. Senior Fang Li, please give me your advice!”

    She looked at you and paused: “…Is there something wrong with your eyes?”

    “Huh?”

    “Oh, no, I saw it wrong.” Kate waved her hand and slapped a piece of paper on your desk.

    Mission Update
    Goal
    Take intern Kate to find the black box of the missing plane.
    You are
    Fang Li, researcher. A graduate student who is about to graduate.
    Clues
    The instruments in this world will display web pages from another world - that is, the articles in this blog.

    What a mess. you think.

    But you stood up anyway.
