0x02 - Hear

2025-08-30

Mission · One Hundred Years 2/3
Goal
Help Cloak learn to listen.
You are a
spectator. There is no "you" in this line - you just see, and the cloak won't learn to see until thousands of years later.
Clue
or 《Two Parts in 100,000》.

Cloak learned to hear before he could see.

At first it could only hear itself. Breathe in, one sound; breathe out, another. The gap between the two breaths became longer and longer, and something else began to squeeze in in the gap - not a sound, but a vibration. Wood, wings, water.

It couldn’t tell what those were. It only remembers texture: quick, slow, dry, sticky.

So it did a very stupid thing: put two shocks into its body at the same time to see if they would smash each other.

No. It puts in a third one.

When the fourth one came in, the first one was squeezed out. It hurriedly chased it, but when it caught up with it, it was already something else.

**It wants to know how many it can accommodate at the same time. **

K-1647 / MODULES
Tip 1 · Direction

The voice in K-1647’s head is called a “module” in the novel.

Tip 2 · Method

Look for the sentence “The modules have been confirmed to be able to communicate at the same time” in “Two out of 100,000”.

Tip 3 · Almost the answer

A two-digit number. Some say it is the answer to life, the universe and everything.

Too much, or too little. The voices in the cloak’s body were a mess.

Forty-two. They were both quiet and loud at the same time.

Cloak thought that was all.

Then the earth began to tremble until it could no longer be heard.
