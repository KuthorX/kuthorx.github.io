The cloak learns to listen before it sees.

At first, it can only hear itself breathing. The farther apart the two breaths are, the more violent vibrations it can hear. Occasionally, these vibrations resonate with other beings.

The cloak could not see what those beings were, and could only remember their voices: hurried, soothing, dry, viscous.

One day, the cloak thought: Instead of listening one by one, let many voices speak in your body at the same time.

It tries to open one, another.

It wants to know: How many sounds can you accommodate at the same time?
