2025-08-30

Inner voices

Colored sentences are voices in the story. Click one to see who is speaking.

    Task · Answer
    Objective
    Listen to Shang Zheng’s explanation of “Eleven Abstract Cases”.
    You are a
    spectator. You are on the grass, a few steps away from them. The thunder is getting closer.
    Clues
    A holographic photo, and an instrument that can rotate colors; and " Game Introduction" .

    1

    “Shang Zheng, you seem to have lost another friend.”

    “It’s not that I lost him, it’s that we were not on the same journey.”

    Lu Yu listened. The wind blew across the grass, casting ripples on it.

    “There is a problem with stream-of-consciousness writing,” Shang Zheng said. “It makes people think that I am sinking into nothingness and moaning for no reason. But it is actually just a mirror.”

    “Isms are meaningless, what is meaningful is feelings. I can say that this grassland is essentially nothingness. This is just an existential question. Some people don’t want to listen, and I can’t help it.”

    “After saying so much, I’m actually just not interested in him.” Lu Yu laughed loudly, “Oh, by the way, has the secret room been solved?”

    “Solved. Not difficult.”

    Thunder sounded in the distance. It’s going to rain.

    “Time is running out.” Lu Yu said, “Before this place turns into a dead elephant again, hurry up and explain the eleven abstract cases to everyone!”

    “Okay.” Shang Zheng smiled as he already knew the answer, “Ignore the heavy stuff for now, let’s talk rationally.”

    2

    Shang Zheng placed a holographic photo on the table. A dense mass of colorful dots, like someone knocking over a box of candy.

    “What is this?” Lu Yu leaned over and said, “There’s nothing.”

    “Nothing.” Shang Zheng corrected him, “These are two different things.”

    He turned the knob underneath the photo. The colors recede layer by layer, leaving only one color.

    “I’ve looked at the ears,” he said. “Now it’s the eyes’ turn.”

    [In this photo, there is nothing in the eyes of people with red-green color blindness. 】

    〖But we can’t see anything! 〗

    Shang Zheng ignored it. He turned the knob one by one, like a radio tuner, with such patience that you wanted to walk away.

    HUE / PHOTO
    Tip 1 · Direction

    Drag the Hue HUE slider. The instrument only lights up one color at a time, while other colors dim.

    Tip 2 · Method

    What are the colors that red-green color blindness cannot see? Turn the slider near that color and slowly adjust it.

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    The slider is around 120° (green). Three words, a question.

    “Look again.” Shang Zheng said.

    **Who is watching? **

    “This is the common problem among the eleven cases.” Shang Zheng said, “In every case, there is someone watching. The person in the black box, the bear in the tavern, the sofa, the audience under the stage… There is another one who is watching everyone all the time.”

    Lu Yu didn’t smile. “You counted ten. Where’s the eleventh?”

    “The eleventh one is not in this plane.” Shang Zheng said, “In another world, someone wrote an introduction to it. Its name is——”

    CASE / 11
    Tip 1 · Direction

    “The Final Case” game introduction in the clue.

    Tip 2 · Method

    Several “planes” were listed in that introduction. Look at the name of plane B.

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    “Someone’s last case.” Someone is Poussin’s daughter, to use her full name.

    Lu Yu shook his head: “That’s not it.”

    “Caitlin Wall’s Final Case.”

    It started to rain. The color of the grass gradually darkened, and there was a faint sea of ​​red in the distance.

    “Are we going to start?” Lu Yu asked.

    “Not yet.” Shang Zheng stood up and patted the grass on his body. “The last case will have to wait until everyone arrives.”
