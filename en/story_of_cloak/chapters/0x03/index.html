Mission · One Hundred Years 3/3
Target
Help Cloak recognize the first symbol it sees.
You are a
spectator. You've been looking at it for thousands of years, and it doesn't know anyone is looking at it.
Clues
In "Two Parts in One Hundred Thousand" , some words have been replaced by strange numbers.

Thousands of years have passed.

The jungle grew taller and taller, green crawling over the cloak’s body and reaching into the blue-white sky.

The moment Cloak opened his eyes, it started raining heavily.

It lowered its head and saw itself in the muddy puddle. There is a symbol floating on the water that it doesn’t recognize yet. It looks like this:

\x1e

Before the cloak learned any words, the symbol was etched into its memory that it could never lose.

The water surface shook, but the symbol did not change.

None.

The first symbol that Cloak recognized was “nothing”.

It raised its head. A furry round piece quickly climbed up the tree and dropped a smaller round piece into a round black hole.

Squirrel, limbs, food, tree hole - it doesn’t know the words yet.

All it knows is that they have shapes, and it never forgets those shapes.

The cloak is gone. The jungle closed in behind it.
