0xA4 - S-Class Secret Room

2025-08-30

Mission·S-Class Secret Room
Target
Enter a secret room with Shang Zheng.
You are a
spectator. You stood behind Shang Zheng; he would occasionally look back at the empty space.
Clues
All the information about the secret room is in this chapter. As for the name of the "murder weapon", it is written in the novel "One in 50,000" .

1

“Half a year, excluding weekends, about 130 days.”

Shang Zheng flipped through the “Researcher’s Manual” and said, “Almost 0.76 A-level secret rooms have to be solved every day…haha.”

“…0.76.” Lu Yu repeated, “Where did that 0.76 come from?”

“One hundred, divided by one hundred and thirty days.”

“You don’t even count this.”

“I don’t even count this.”

Vault Energy Research Institute on the Moon, unknown number of people. People here do the same thing: unlock the secret room randomly generated by the “Chord” system, submit the answer to the system, and if the judgment is correct, the energy will be available.

Shang Zheng is one of them. He didn’t care where the energy came from or where it went - Lu Yu reminded him once that it was a secret that only the “system” knew, so he didn’t ask again.

There is another item written at the back of the manual: 100 Class A secret rooms must be unlocked every six months. If you are not satisfied, you will be transferred; if you are not satisfied again, you will be fired.

A Class A chamber takes a lot of effort. No one can remain productive all the time.

“Either solve S-class, which is more difficult than A-class, or wait to be eliminated.” A message popped up in the hologram.

That’s right.

Letter · A signature file in the chat software

K-0122 Absolute rational person with desires

Appearance

  • Appears to be around 28–35, actual age unknown - not important
  • Pale green pupils with a light gold ring on the edge, showing a faint iridescence under light
  • Dress minimally: playful T-shirts, portable neural recording rings and wrist-worn physiological sensors are always on your body
  • I often wear a grain-free silver ring on my fingertips to slightly focus my thoughts.

#Personality & Motivation

  • MBTI: ENTJ; treat emotions as noise and only reveal “simulated emotions” when necessary to reduce gaming resistance.
  • The value function is clear: maximize long-term structural benefits while maintaining the sustainable supply of its own reward system.
  • Have desires, but do not deny them: indulge in the sense of clarity and conquest brought by dopamine/endorphins, but use reason to abstract desires into “adjustable rewards.”
  • Likes to “witness intellectual conquests” - whether he accomplishes it himself or sees others changing the distribution of group strategies with insights, he will feel “system-level pleasure”.

#believe

  • Dogma that others object to
  • Dogma supported by others

Shang selected an S-class secret room.

“Hey, someone is challenging the S-level right after joining the company??” In the chat software that he had blocked, messages were still being sent.

2·Secret Room

Chord System · S Level · Case File
Decedent
Zhang Tao, investor.
Reporter
Gu Ling, the core developer of the "Chord" log system security module.
Location
Room 3702, Building 9, No. 439, Shibo Avenue.

Ever since young Gu Ling learned the truth about her mother’s death, she secretly vowed to kill Zhang Tao with her own hands one day. However, after many years, she never waited for that day.

At this time, at 4:14 pm, she just opened the door of a locked room and found a body lying in the room.

After five minutes, Gu Ling took out her mobile phone and dialed the number of the police station.

Judging from the corpse’s appearance, the deceased died of natural gas poisoning. However, when Gu Ling first entered the house, she did not feel suffocated due to the high methane concentration.

The monitoring log at the door shoe cabinet is as follows:

12:10 客厅大门开启。
12:10 客厅大门关闭。
12:48 客厅大门开启。
12:48 客厅大门关闭。
12:50 天然气开启。
13:50 天然气关闭。
16:12 客厅大门开启。

Gu Ling thought about all the possibilities:

  1. The murderer turned on natural gas and put on oxygen supply equipment before closing the door - the order of the logs is wrong.
  2. The murderer entered and exited through the floor-to-ceiling windows - window switches also leave records.
  3. The logs are not trustworthy - she is the developer of the system security module, which is almost impossible.
  4. Natural gas can be controlled remotely – this apartment can only be remotely turned off, not turned on remotely.
  5. Zhang Tao committed suicide - he cherished his life like a miser cherishing his money, which is impossible.
  6. The murderer is still at the scene - police officer Fu Huang checked the bedroom and bathroom. There was no one there and there was no place to hide anyone.

————————

In the holograph, when the case was replayed here, Shang Zheng took off one of his headphones.

There was a low ringing in my ears.

He turned the holocron’s volume down to zero. It’s still there.

He glanced at his colleague at the workstation next to him. The other person was drinking a refreshing drink and had no reaction.

“…I see.” Shang Zheng said, “This secret room was not originally prepared for people who can’t hear it.”

CHORD / S-CLASS · 1
Tip 1 · Direction

Gu Ling did not smell or suffocate from methane when she entered the room. Natural gas may be just a cover-up.

Tip 2 · Method

Six possibilities have been ruled out. What Shang Zheng noticed was something that no one else noticed - he took off his headphones.

Tip 3 · Almost the answer

An inaudible “voice” that can kill people. Two words, the first word is “sound”.

“The answer is invalid.” The system replied emotionlessly.

Shang Zheng wrote in his reasoning: Natural gas is a cover-up, and the cause of death is an aggressive sound wave.

The system asked him to complete one item: Name of the murder weapon.

Shang Zheng remembered a novel. An interstellar court, an unprecedented case, a sound wave with “a 1 in 50,000 probability of killing humans.”

CHORD / S-CLASS · 2
Tip 1 · Direction

The answer lies in the novel “One in 50,000”.

Tip 2 · Method

In Chapter One, the prosecutor introduces the name of this sound wave to the court.

Tip 3 · Almost the answer

Write K-1647 upside down.

“The name of the murder weapon does not exist.” The system said.

“7461-K.” Shang Zheng submitted his reasoning.

The system did not ask who the murderer was. Shang Zheng didn’t write it either.

He knows why this question is S-level: Those who can solve it must first hear it; and most of those who hear it will not survive.

He is the exception. As for why, he wasn’t going to tell anyone.

After the submission was completed, Shang Zheng took off the hologram and went to experience other free things in the institute.

Because he knows that soon, these things will no longer be free.
