0xA4 - S-Class Locked Room

2025-08-30
Mission · S-Class Locked Room
Goal
Solve an S-class locked-room case for Zhi Shang and submit the answer.
You are
An observer. The protagonist is new lunar energy-researcher Zhi Shang.
Clue
All case information appears in this chapter. The weapon’s name is written in One in Fifty Thousand.

I

The Locked-Room Energy Institute stands on the Moon. Researchers solve rooms randomly generated by the Chord system; correct answers produce energy. Zhi Shang ignores where the energy comes from. Every six months he must solve 100 A-class rooms or be transferred and eventually dismissed.

He chooses an S-class room.

Letter · K-0122, The Desiring Absolute Rationalist

K-0122 maximizes long-term structural gain while treating desire as a tunable reward term and emotion as negotiable noise.

II · The locked room

Chord System · S-Class · Case File
Victim
Zhang Tao, investor.
Reporter
Gu Ling, core developer of the Chord log system’s security module.
Location
Room 3702, Building 9, 439 Expo Avenue.

Gu Ling once vowed to kill Zhang Tao after learning the truth about her mother’s death. Years later, she finds his body in a locked room at 4:14 p.m. The apparent cause is methane poisoning, but she entered without suffocating.

The logs, ventilation, lock, and timing form an S-class puzzle. Zhi Shang reconstructs the sequence, identifies the weapon named in One in Fifty Thousand , and submits the solution to Chord.

The system accepts the answer and releases energy. The case is solved, but the final record hints that the Moon institute itself is part of a larger locked room.
