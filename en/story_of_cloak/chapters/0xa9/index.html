    Task · Little Black Box
    Target
    “Audit” three small black boxes. Kate says it's work.
    You are an employee of
    a company with the nickname “Socrates”. The boss is Kate.
    Clues
    A case handled by Kate’s father: "Six Answers - Necklace 2" .

    “‘Socrates’!!!” An earth-shattering howl.

    You were disturbed. The person calling you is Kate, your boss.

    “What is going on??!!” She slapped a small black box on your desk. Nothing could be seen inside.

    “Isn’t this just a little black box?”

    “Nonsense! This is an audit! Review and calculation! We call it μέγα τύπος!”

    “…large model?”

    “Yes, yes! Or is it called μέγα παράδειγμα?”

    You know something she doesn’t: there’s no word for “mata” in ancient Greek. But you didn’t say it.

    You think about it for a moment, and then you realize something: You know you know nothing.

    You smiled.

    “What are you laughing at, ‘Socrates’? Don’t think that having a fancy name means you’re really a philosopher!”

    〖Do you want to talk back? Talk back! 〗

    【No. 】

    / You two go aside. /

    / Listen up. Three principles of communication: 1. Control your emotions; 2. Change at least three ways of saying things; 3. Never stop trying. /

    You took a deep breath.

    “Okay, sorry, Kate. I’ll do the audit.”

    “…Okay.” Kate sighed, “We don’t have much time. We must find the Green Eye, and scientific research needs money.”

    She also slapped two identical small black boxes on the table. There are three in total, labeled A, B, and C.

    “There is only one box that contains clues to the Green Eyes. There is a sentence posted on each box, but only one sentence is true. My father said a sentence, do you know?”

    AUDIT / QUOTE
    Tip 1 · Direction

    Kate’s father is detective Person Wall. This sentence appears in “The Six-fold Answer: Necklace 2”.

    Tip 2 · Method

    Look for “rules out other possibilities” in the article.

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    Five words, meaning “hard to believe”.

    “You CiiaKua!” Kate said.

    “…Unbelievable.” Kate repeated, “Okay, let’s start ruling it out.”

    Three small black boxes are labeled:

    Three little black boxes · Only one sentence is true
    A
    “The clue isn’t here.”
    B
    “The clue is in A.”
    C
    “The clue isn’t here.”
    AUDIT / BOX
    Tip 1 · Direction

    Hypotheses one by one: If the clue is in A, are each of the three sentences true or false?

    Tip 2 · Method

    If in A: B is true, C is true - two sentences are true, no. If in B: A is true, C is true - that doesn’t work either.

    Tip 3 · Almost the answer

    Only the last box is left.

    You open the box. Empty. Kate glares at you.

    C。

    You opened C.

    There is nothing inside.

    Kate was silent for one fifty thousandth of a second.

    “…That’s right.” She actually smiled, “If the Green Eye could be put into a box, I would have found it long ago.”

    At that moment, you vaguely saw Kate’s eyes burning, pale green.

    “Don’t be in a daze, ‘Socrates.’” Kate pushed the three empty boxes aside. “Don’t belittle yourself, and don’t be too ambitious. You have potential.”

    …Schrödinger’s little black box.

    It’s pretty interesting.

    With that in mind, you started exploring .
