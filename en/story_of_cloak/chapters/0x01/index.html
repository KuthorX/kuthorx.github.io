0x01 - Breathing

2025-08-30
Mission · One Hundred Years 1/3
target
help the cloak learn to breathe.
You are a
bystander. The protagonist of this line is a cloak.
clue
cloak is a kind of existence called K-1647. Instructions for K-1647, written in the novel Two Hundred Thousandths .

A shabby Cloak appears in the spring jungle.

The shape inside the cloak was erratic. It has no self and no voice.

“Where is this place?” the cloak asked in its heart.

There are lots of moving things in the jungle: butterflies flying by, birds singing overhead, bugs in the dirt. The cloak is interested in them, but it doesn’t feel them yet.

It can only do one thing: * * absorb time * *.

The cloak fixed the eyes and turned each moment into a point in the mind. These points are where it spent its time, and they are also moments worth remembering.

It doesn’t know if it will suddenly disappear one day. K-1647 has a problem: every day, there is a very small probability that it will stop working on its own.

The cloak was just absorbing day by day.

K-1647 / 100 YEARS
Tip 1 · Direction

This is a probability problem, but you don’t have to calculate it yourself. The answer is in a novel.

Tip 2 · Method

Open “Two Hundred Thousandths” and look for the passage that says “100 years”.

Tip 3 · Almost the answer

A percentage, 48 o’clock.

The cloak’s breathing stopped and continued.

Less than half.

The cloak survived.

By the time it realized that it could breathe freely and feel the fresh air in its nose, time had passed * * a hundred years * * .
