0x01 - Breathing

2025-08-30

Mission · One Hundred Years 1/3
Goal
Help Cloak learn to breathe.
You are a
spectator. You have no body, and you are not in this jungle - but you have not missed a second of the hundred years that the cloak has absorbed.
Clue
The cloak is an entity called K-1647. The description of K-1647 is written in the novel "Two Parts in One Hundred Thousand" .

A shabby cloak appeared in the spring jungle.

The form inside the cloak was erratic. It has no self and no sound yet.

“Where is this?” Cloak asked in his mind.

There are many moving things in the jungle: butterflies flying by, birds chirping overhead, and bugs in the soil. Cloak is interested in them, but can’t feel them yet.

It can only do one thing: absorb time.

The cloak freezes the eyes, turning every moment into a point in the mind. These points are places where it has been and moments worth remembering.

It doesn’t know if it will suddenly disappear one day. K-1647 has a problem: every day, there is a very small probability that it will stop running on its own.

The cloak is just taking it in day by day.

K-1647 / 100 YEARS
Tip 1 · Direction

This is a probability question, but you don’t have to do the math yourself. The answer is written in a novel.

Tip 2 · Method

Open “Two Parts in 100,000” and look for the paragraph that says “100 years.”

Tip 3 · Almost the answer

A percentage, 48 points.

Cloak’s breathing paused, then resumed.

Less than half.

The cloak survived.

By the time it realizes that it can breathe freely and feel the fresh air in its nose, time has passed One Hundred Years .
