    As the tavern rose in the rain, I thought about the day I first saw the earfish.

    It was also raining that day.

    That place is called Dead Elephant . An old elephant was looked at by the Green Eyes before it died, and its body became a place. The rain there is dark red and never stops.

    I’m looking for something.

    I’m looking for the Green Eyes. But I am a cat, and cats are red-green blind. I have never seen green in my life.

    “What are you doing here?” A big bear asked me, standing in the rain.

    “Look for the Green Eyes.”

    “You can’t see green, you can’t find anything green.”

    “…I have no choice.” I said, “My Husburd is gone. My daughter, I can’t find it either. I still have three children at home, who are staying with my sister.”

    The bear’s eyes are pale green. I can’t see it, but I know it – that’s what a lot of people say.

    He didn’t answer me right away. The rainwater flowed down his broken ear and into the scars on his body.

    I suddenly noticed something: when he was talking, his nose didn’t move at all.
