Story of Cloak · WEB PUZZLE

The Green Eyes

A web puzzle game hidden in this blog

You play as Fang Li, a researcher. One day you wake up and find yourself in a parallel world in 2014.

The instrument in that world displays the page in this blog. Read them, find the answers, and put the answers back into the story.

Start game Continue:

How to play

  • Clues are in other articles/videos on this blog (links will be given in the story).
  • Fill in the answer in the input box in the story and press Enter to submit.
  • Wrong answers will be counted. Answer the question incorrectly 3 times to unlock the first hint, and then get closer and closer to the answer.
  • Progress only exists in your browser. You can "Replay this page" at the bottom of each page.

Voices

More than one voice speaks in the story. Each uses a different bracket:

  • 【…】 cloak · examine Calm, like a manual. What is said is usually believable.
  • 〖…〗 emotions · emotions Talkative and emotional are the panicked parts of you.
  • / … / Grandmaster · bystander Rarely speaks. When talking, everyone will be quiet.
