[D-XXXX] Dead Elephant

2025-08-30 Green Eyes-Character Card

An old elephant was stared at by green eyes before dying, and his body was transformed into a place.

This place is chaotic in time and space, and cultural symbols from different places blend with each other to create different worlds.

It is said that in this place, people who are afraid of death can see a red sea and a rain that will never stop.

A bear-shaped man named Earfish occasionally travels here, but he always notices a cat-shaped man named Fire Claw.

“What are you doing here?”

“Look for the ‘Green Eyes’.”

“You’re a cat, you’re red-green blind, you can’t find green things.”

“…but I have no choice. Can you tell me how to find it?”

“Why?”

“Because I should be like you, someone who has been stared at by green eyes.”

“That’s not a reason.”

“Kate Wall is my daughter, and I can’t find her. Maybe the Green Eyes can help me find her.”

“I can travel through time and space, but it’s not enough. We can only wait for the next person to appear.”

“How long do we have to wait?”

“I don’t know.”

“Okay, then I’ll wait here until I’m alive next time.”

This is the legend of the dead elephant, and if you are willing to believe it, then life may not be so painful.
