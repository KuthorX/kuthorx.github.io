[A-4597] EarFish

2025-08-30 The Green Eyes - Character Cards

Appearance

  • Species: bear.
  • Fur: brown, yellow, and red, mainly brown.
  • Eyes: sage green.
  • Ears: one injured bear ear; the other is fan-shaped and closer to a fish gill.

Hearing and respiration

  • Though EarFish has a normal bear’s nose, breathing is actually done through the ears.
  • Because the ears must also handle hearing, more kinds of sound can be heard.

Identity | clothing

In 2442, EarFish runs the tavern in 0x04 , wearing a bartender uniform.

EarFish once wore cargo pants and work clothes to support two children. After they became independent, EarFish opened a tavern.

In section 2 of 0x07 , EarFish wears a tattered white tunic and assorted tribal ornaments.

Skills

  • Can travel through time and space.
  • Cannot change objective facts, especially facts already written down.

Notes

  • A regular at the Dead Elephant. There, EarFish met Burning Claws and agreed to carry her a dagger and a message.
  • Knows many things, but rarely speaks.
  • Appears in 0x04 The Tavern and 0x07 Dead Elephant .
