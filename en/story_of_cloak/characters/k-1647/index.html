[K-1647] Cloak Cloak

2025-08-30 Green Eyes-Character Card

#Basic description

External composition

  1. Material composition: half carbon-based and half silicon-based life.
  2. Appearance: The head shape has feline characteristics.
  3. Clothing: Cloak with hood, see the [Clothing] section for details.

Internal definition

  1. Behavioral motivation: Transformed from somewhere, in order to find his own definition, he began to come into contact with carbon-based life.
  2. Don’t be hungry but know how to enjoy food.

#clothing

About the cloak

  1. dilapidated.
  2. Color changeable. By default, it is the same color as the wearer, and the cloak comes with a hood.
  3. The form inside the cloak is slender and cat-like by default, but will change according to the carbon-based life forms it has been exposed to in the past 7 days.

About the hood

  1. The hood has an outline that fits the shape of the cat’s ears. Generally, the hood is not worn.
  2. You need to come into contact with carbon-based life during the day (actually even if you come into contact with the earth), additional intelligence will be generated during the contact, which will be temporarily stored in the hood.
  3. You need to wear a hood at night to digest the knowledge accumulated during the day.

external connections

  1. Place of birth: Daytime in a jungle somewhere.
  2. Date of birth: unknown.

Supplement · Voice in the head

  • The K-1647 model has multiple “modules” inside, up to 42 of which can communicate at the same time (see “Two in 100,000” ).
  • In the story, they speak in different brackets: [ ] is the cloak, which is the inspection module; 〖〗 is the emotion module; / … / is the master.
  • Appearance: 0x01 Breath .
