[K-1647] Cloak

2025-08-30 The Green Eyes - Character Cards

Basic description

Physical composition

  1. Half carbon-based, half silicon-based life.
  2. A head with feline features.
  3. A hooded cloak; see the Clothing section.

Inner definition

  1. Transformed from somewhere unknown, it began contacting carbon-based life in search of its own definition.
  2. Does not get hungry, but understands how to enjoy food.

Clothing

The cloak

  1. Worn and old.
  2. Color-changing. By default it matches the wearer’s color range and includes a hood.
  3. The form beneath the cloak is normally slender and covered with catlike fur, but changes with the forms of carbon-based life encountered during the previous seven days.

The hood

  1. Its outline follows the wearer’s cat ears. The hood is usually down.
  2. During the day, contact with carbon-based life produces extra knowledge, temporarily stored in the hood.
  3. At night, the hood must be worn to digest the knowledge gathered during the day.

External connections

  1. Birthplace: daytime in an unknown jungle.
  2. Birth time: unknown.

Notes · voices inside the mind

  • A K-1647 model contains multiple modules; up to 42 can communicate at once (see Two in One Hundred Thousand ).
  • In the story they speak with different brackets: 【】 is Cloak, the observation module; 〖〗 is the emotion module; /…/ is the master.
  • Appears in 0x01 Breathing .
