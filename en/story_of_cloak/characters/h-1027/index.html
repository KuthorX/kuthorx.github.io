2025-08-30 The Green Eyes - Character Cards

Before I could say “that’s it”

We often do not know how to express ourselves politely.

By the time we truly can express ourselves politely,

we find that we have grown so far apart.

To keep the signs from becoming obvious, we choose to hide our true selves.

And only when

Yesterday’s Sakura

the flowers

fall

do we realize

that so much has already passed,

long ago, like letters,

beyond regret,

leaving us weeping.
